How it works

An intent that settles in the real world

On NEAR Intents you say what you want, and competing solvers deliver it. Sendam does the same for a Nigerian electricity bill, with local agents as the solvers.

You lock USDC, from anywhere

Choose the meter and the amount, and set the rate you are offering. The USDC waits in a NEAR contract, not with us and not with an agent.

A local agent pays in naira

Agents who hold naira and want dollars pick the request up and buy the token from the electricity company.

The lights come on

Once the purchase is confirmed, the agent is paid and your family opens a link to collect the token. No wallet, no app.

Why it is safe

The contract only ever pays two people

The agent named in a confirmed claim, or the sender who funded the request. Nobody else can move the money, including us.

If you are sending

  • Charged only on proofThe agent is paid after the electricity company confirms the purchase, never before.
  • A refund you cannot be deniedIf nobody pays by your deadline, anyone can trigger the refund, and it can only go back to you.
  • The token stays privateOnly a fingerprint of your family's link is on chain. The token is fetched with that link, never published.

If you are an agent

  • A reserved jobTaking a request holds it for you for 30 minutes, so nobody else pays the same bill.
  • Paid even if you run lateIf your hold lapses but nobody else took the request, a confirmed payment still pays you.
  • Never pay twiceEvery purchase has a fixed reference, so a retry looks the payment up instead of buying again.
For agents

Earn dollars for something you already do

POS agents and bill vendors across Nigeria sell electricity tokens all day. Sendam turns that into a way to earn USDC, with the rate and tip on every request set in the open.
