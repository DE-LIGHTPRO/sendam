How it works

One bill, three people.

Like NEAR Intents, you say what you want and someone competes to deliver it. Here the delivery is a prepaid electricity token, and the someone is a local agent with naira.

01

Ada, abroad

Locks USDC for her mother's meter and sets the rate she is offering. The money waits in a NEAR contract.

02

Tunde, the agent

Takes the request, buys the token from the electricity company in naira, and is paid in USDC once it is confirmed.

03

Mum, at home

Opens a link, sees the payment arrive, and types the token into her meter. No wallet, no app.

Guarantees

The money only goes two places.

To the agent named in a confirmed claim, or back to the person who sent it. Nobody else can move it, including us.

01

Charged only on proof

The agent is paid after the electricity company confirms the purchase, never before.

02

A refund nobody can block

If no one pays by your deadline, anyone can trigger the refund, and it can only go back to you.

03

One agent per bill

Taking a request reserves it for 30 minutes, so two agents never pay the same meter.

04

The token stays private

Only a fingerprint of the family's link is on chain. The token itself is never published.
