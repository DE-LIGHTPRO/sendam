How it works

An intent that settles in the real world

On NEAR Intents, you say what you want and competing solvers deliver it. Sendam does the same for a Nigerian electricity bill, with local agents as the solvers.

Step 1

Abroad, you lock USDC

Choose the meter and the amount. The USDC sits in a NEAR contract, not with us and not with an agent.

Step 2

A local agent pays in naira

Agents who already hold naira and want dollars pick up the request and pay the electricity company.

Step 3

The token reaches home

Once the payment is confirmed, the agent is paid and your family collects the token from a link. No wallet needed.

Why it is safe

The contract only ever pays two people

The agent named in a confirmed claim, or the sender who funded the request. Nobody else can move the money.

For the sender

  • Charged only on proofThe agent is paid after the electricity company confirms the purchase, not before.
  • Automatic refundIf no agent pays by the deadline, anyone can trigger the refund, and it can only go back to you.
  • The token stays privateOnly a fingerprint of your family's link is on chain. The token is fetched with the link, never published.

For the agent

  • A reserved jobTaking a request holds it for you for 30 minutes, so nobody else pays the same bill.
  • Paid even if you are slowIf your claim lapses but nobody else has taken it, a confirmed payment still pays you.
  • No double paymentsEvery purchase has a fixed reference, so a retry looks the payment up instead of paying twice.
For agents

Earn dollars for something you already do

POS agents and bill vendors across Nigeria already sell electricity tokens all day. Sendam turns that into a way to earn USDC, with the rate and tip set in the open by each request.
